Feeds for hens

In Ghana, there are many types of chicken feed depending on the bird's age and purpose. Some are designed for hens, some for chicks, and some for roosters and mixed flocks.

Quick nutrition note

  • Young birds need more protein.
  • Layers need calcium for strong eggshells.
  • Broilers need energy-rich feed for growth.

Common chicken feed types

Feed Type Target Age Protein Level Main Focus
Chick Starter 0 to 6 weeks 20%–21% Rapid early growth, organ development, and feather growth.
Grower Feed 6 to 28 weeks 16%–18% Steady frame growth without premature sexual development.
Layer Feed 18+ weeks or first egg 16%–18% Maintenance and calcium support for strong eggshells.
Broiler / Finisher 3 to 8 weeks 18%–20% Energy and protein blend for rapid muscle gain.
Flock Raiser / All-Flock Any age with free-choice calcium 18%–20% Multi-purpose option for mixed flocks including hens, roosters, and ducks.

Clean water

Always provide clean, fresh water to prevent dehydration and poor growth.

Balanced feed

Use feed that matches the bird's age and purpose for better health.

Healthy flock

Proper nutrition helps chickens stay strong and productive.
